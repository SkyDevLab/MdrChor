Free MDR Calculator & Payment Split Calculator

MDRChor helps you calculate merchant discount rate (MDR) charges on payment transactions and compare them with a split-payment approach. Enter an amount to see the original MDR, individual transaction charges, total charge after splitting, and the displayed potential savings.

How does MDR calculation work?

This calculator uses the example rules displayed above: transactions up to ₹2,000 have no charge, amounts above ₹2,000 through ₹75,000 use a 0.4% calculation, and amounts above ₹75,000 use a ₹300 fixed charge.

Example: ₹10,000 payment

At 0.4%, a single ₹10,000 transaction produces a ₹40 example MDR charge. With Automatic mode, MDRChor can show five ₹2,000 transactions, each with no charge under the configured rules. Custom mode lets you choose another split count, such as four transactions: ₹2,000 + ₹2,000 + ₹2,000 + ₹4,000. In that example, the 0.4% calculation applies to the ₹4,000 transaction.

Why use a payment split calculator?

  • Compare a single payment with multiple transaction amounts.
  • See exactly where an MDR charge is applied.
  • Test custom split counts before making a calculation.
  • Understand the difference between original MDR and split MDR.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MDR?

MDR stands for Merchant Discount Rate, a fee associated with eligible payment transactions. Actual rates and conditions depend on the payment provider and applicable agreement.

Is MDRChor a real payment processor?

No. MDRChor is a browser-based calculator. It does not process, route, authorize, or settle payments.

Can I choose custom splits?

Yes. Select Custom and enter a split count. The calculator keeps the input editable and calculates the resulting transaction amounts and charges.

Does splitting always remove MDR?

No. MDRChor only applies the rules configured in the calculator. Actual provider fees, limits, taxes, minimum charges, and transaction policies can differ.

How much is 0.4% of ₹10,000?

0.4% of ₹10,000 is ₹40.00.
